Ancient Wonders

Stone that has outlived empires

01

Pyramid of Djoser

Saqqara, Giza

  1. Built about 4,650 years ago for King Djoser of the Third Dynasty.
  2. The architect Imhotep is credited with its design.
  3. Its six stacked steps rise to around 60 meters.
02 Before & After . Page 1

Temple of Hatshepsut

Deir el-Bahari, Luxor

  1. Built in the 15th century BC for Hatshepsut, a woman who ruled as pharaoh.
  2. Its three terraces rise in front of towering limestone cliffs.
  3. Its wall reliefs record a trading expedition to the Land of Punt.
03

Dendera Temple

Qena

  1. It is dedicated to Hathor, goddess of love, music and joy.
  2. The main temple standing today dates largely to the late Ptolemaic period.
  3. The famous Dendera Zodiac came from its roof and is now in the Louvre.

Faith & Knowledge

Places of prayer and learning

04

Saint Catherine’s Monastery

South Sinai

  1. It stands at the foot of Mount Sinai.
  2. It was built in the 6th century by order of Emperor Justinian.
  3. It became part of a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2002.
05

Al-Azhar Mosque

Historic Cairo

  1. The Fatimids began building it in 970 AD.
  2. It was the first mosque built in the new city of Cairo.
  3. Al-Azhar University, which grew from it, is one of the oldest in the world.
06

Bibliotheca Alexandrina

Alexandria

  1. It opened in 2002 in memory of the ancient Library of Alexandria.
  2. The Norwegian firm Snøhetta led its architectural design.
  3. It has shelf space for up to eight million books.

South & West

Riverside temples and desert horizons

07

Edfu Temple

Edfu, Aswan

Page 2
  1. It is dedicated to Horus, the falcon-headed god.
  2. It was built between 237 and 57 BC.
  3. It is one of the best preserved temples in Egypt.
08

Siwa Oasis

Matrouh

  1. It lies in the Western Desert near the Libyan border.
  2. Alexander the Great visited its Oracle of Amun in 331 BC.
  3. It is known for its salt lakes, dates and olives.
09

White Desert

Near Farafra

  1. Its landscape is famous for bright white chalk rock.
  2. Wind and sand carved the rocks into mushroom-like shapes.
  3. It was declared a protected area in 2002.
